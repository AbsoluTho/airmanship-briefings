# Airmanship Briefings

A flight preparation aid built on Airbus FCTM concepts. Import the Mission Plus OFP and NOTAM PDFs, work through the Dispatch Briefing in form order, check tankering and underload, run the cabin crew, departure, arrival and jumpseat briefings, and keep the phases of flight and normal checklists at hand. Up to four sectors.

- Works in any modern browser and installs as an app on iPhone, iPad and Android.
- Works offline after the first visit.
- Everything you type, and every PDF you import, stays on your device. Nothing is uploaded unless you sign in with the optional Cloud sync (Google sign-in) to share your data between your own devices.

## Install on a phone or tablet

- **iPhone / iPad:** open the page in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
- **Android:** open the page in Chrome, tap the menu, then Install app (or Add to Home screen).

## Important

This is an unofficial preparation aid. It does not replace the FCOM, FCTM, the operator's OM-A or any company procedure, and it is not affiliated with any airline. Verify every figure against approved sources.
